# R&D Ratchet article-hero art direction

Date: 2026-07-24  
Output: Seventeen distinct panoramic editorial illustrations for the article hero position.  
Repository target: `assets/images/rd-ratchet/articles/`

## Shared visual grammar

- Bright editorial-scientific illustration rather than dark cinematic concept art.
- Wide panoramic composition designed for an approximately 2:1 crop.
- Warm paper or softly colored architectural background with visible ink, gouache, screen-print, or technical-drawing texture.
- A thin illuminated thread recurs as a symbol of retained knowledge; fine connecting lines recur as institutional adjacencies.
- No title, labels, logos, trademarks, signatures, watermarks, UI copy, or pseudo-text inside the image.
- Historical people may be recognizable, but the images are interpretive editorial illustrations rather than documentary reconstructions.
- Technical objects should be recognizable and plausible without suggesting access to restricted facilities or confidential programs.
- Avoid generic glowing-brain imagery, corporate stock-photo aesthetics, oppressive darkness, visual clutter, and illegible generated typography.

## Review criteria

For every candidate:

1. The principal symbols are immediately visible at article-hero scale.
2. The composition survives a wide 2:1 center crop.
3. The image is materially brighter than the original shared hero.
4. No accidental text, logo, signature, or watermark appears.
5. Human faces, hands, instruments, and technical objects do not contain distracting generation defects.
6. Neighboring articles do not share the same dominant palette or composition.
7. The alt text and caption describe the finished image rather than an unrealized prompt.
